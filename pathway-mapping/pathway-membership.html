Methods & changelog

Versionv2 — rebuilt from COMPLETE canonical SMPDB member lists (every member fingerprinted via PubChem), matched two independent ways and unioned.
MethodPer pathway: deterministic InChIKey/CID fingerprint join AND independent agent reasoning, on the identical canonical member list; membership = union.
Confidencehigh = exact InChIKey/CID identity (found by the algorithm). low = agent-only 'detected form / parent' derivative call (e.g. pyroglutamate↔glutamate) — review before use.
Head-to-head countoriginal run = 25 distinct compounds; deterministic (canonical) = 24; agent (canonical) = 27; UNION (this file) = 27.
Who wonAgent > deterministic (27 vs 24): the agent caught 3 derivative matches the fingerprint join cannot see, and missed nothing the algorithm found. Recall was not the problem.
Added vs v1Cytidine; L-Glutamate; L-Pyroglutamic acid/5-Oxo-proline; N-Acetylglucosaminitol (note: 'L-Glutamate' is a duplicate-name entry of L-Glutamic acid — same molecule, not a new one).
Removed vs v14-Guanidinobutyric acid; gamma-Aminobutyric acid (GABA) — these are NOT members of the canonical SMPDB Arginine & Proline set; v1 included them as medium-confidence judgement calls, now corrected out.
Why only ~27Your 388 are ~330 plant secondary metabolites (flavonoids, glycosides, lipids) plus ~40 amino-acids/amines, ~7 organic acids, ~6 nucleosides, ~5 sugars. Only the central-metabolism subset can belong to these 13 amino-acid/central pathways, so ~27 is the real ceiling — not an under-count.
CofactorsCanonical sets include ubiquitous cofactors (water, CO2, ATP, NAD, phosphate, THF...). They appear under 'Coverage gaps' because they are not in your 388.
